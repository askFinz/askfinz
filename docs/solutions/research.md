## Frequently asked

### How does askFinz handle citations in research notebooks?

Citations are first-class in Research notebooks. Every claim the workspace generates attaches a reference to the source document, passage or article. The trail from conclusion to evidence is part of the structure, not a footnote added at the end.

### Can I import my own papers, datasets and prior notes?

Yes. Upload documents to Storage and they become part of the searchable index Research reads from. Semantic Search finds the relevant passage across your whole corpus — not just the file name.

### Is askFinz suitable for academic publishing workflows?

It suits the research, synthesis and drafting stages well. askFinz doesn't submit to journals or manage peer review — it produces the notebook and the cited draft you then format for submission. Your reference manager and editorial workflow stay separate.
