## Frequently asked

### Does askFinz replace our observability or incident management stack?

No. askFinz doesn't replace Datadog, PagerDuty or your CI runner. The workshop produces the pipeline, transformer, dashboard and snapshot you would otherwise stitch together by hand — the systems your platform team already trusts stay where they are.

### Does the Train workspace require deep ML expertise?

A working understanding of fine-tuning helps. Train is built for teams who want to adapt a base model to domain data without standing up their own training cluster — you don't need to be an ML researcher, but you should know what you're training toward.

### Which operations workspaces are shipping today?

Code, Workflow, Storage, Chat, Train and Projects are in private access now. Charts and Data are on the roadmap. More first-party MCP connectors for common platform tools are landing inside Workflow as they ship.
