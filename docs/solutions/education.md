## Frequently asked

### Will askFinz write a student's assignment for them?

The workshop is Socratic by design — it asks for the source, pushes for the alternative reading, and works alongside the student rather than instead of them. The trail of the conversation is visible and makes a useful assessment artefact in itself.

### Does askFinz work with our existing LMS?

Yes, for course material. Learn imports courses from Canvas, Brightspace, Moodle and Blackboard — lectures, slides and readings are read page by page and become the source the study tools are built from. Artefacts made elsewhere in the workshop still export from Storage and are uploaded back manually; there is no grade write-back.

### How does assessment work?

Learn writes a timed exam from your own course material, grades it on the server, applies browser-level proctoring and gives an AI debrief on every attempt. Progress analytics build from the real attempts, so weak topics and exam readiness come from what a student actually did rather than from self-report.

### Is askFinz suited to a whole class or just individual teachers?

Both. Teachers use Research and Storage to build lesson artefacts, and Learn to turn a course into study and assessment tools. Students work on their own threads at their own pace, each with their own progress record.
