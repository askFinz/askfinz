## Frequently asked

### Does askFinz work alongside my existing data terminal?

Yes. askFinz doesn't replace your data terminal, order-management system or book of record. You bring the filings, transcripts and spreadsheet exports; the workshop reads, cites and drafts from them. Positions and trade routing stay where they are.

### Can I use my firm's house templates for research notes?

Yes. Upload your template to Storage and the workshop will apply it as the output format for new notes and briefings, keeping your IC-presentable layout consistent across the book.

### Is there a difference between the cloud workspace and the Desktop App for wealth teams?

The cloud workspaces — Research, News, Search, Storage, Chat — are the primary surface for wealth work, and they are what is running today. The Desktop App brings its own web browser and askFinz Drive to your machine, with the development environment following; most wealth analysts start with the cloud plan.
