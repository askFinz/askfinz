## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz monitor competitors automatically?

Live multi-source news clusters surface competitor announcements as they happen, deduped so one story doesn't read four ways, and you keep a standing notebook that accumulates the detail over time.

### Does it build a battlecard?

The workspace drafts the comparison from the sources you've threaded — where a rival is ahead and where they're exposed — with each claim linked back to where it came from.

### Where does the competitor information come from?

From public sources you bring or follow — filings, announcements and multi-source news — synthesised in one place with citations, not from any private or proprietary feed.
