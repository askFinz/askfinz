## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz search case law and legislation?

Yes. Judgments and legislation are part of the index, searchable by the legal issue rather than only by case name or section number, with each result carrying its formal citation.

### Can I bring my own matter files into the research?

Yes. Upload the filings, correspondence and prior memos for a matter and they join a searchable notebook alongside the case law and legislation you pull in.

### Is this a substitute for legal advice or a qualified lawyer?

No. askFinz produces sourced research and a searchable record — it doesn't give legal advice, file anything, or replace the judgement of a qualified lawyer.
