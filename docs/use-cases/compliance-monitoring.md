## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz monitor regulatory changes automatically?

Deduped, multi-source news clusters surface enforcement actions and rule changes as they're reported, and a standing notebook accumulates the detail on a name or a regulation over time, so a review starts from an up-to-date file rather than a blank one.

### Can I search across filings and guidance I've collected?

Yes. Upload the documents you track and they join a searchable index, so you can find the specific clause or line rather than re-reading the whole document.

### Does askFinz make compliance determinations?

No. It surfaces the filings, the guidance and the news with sources attached — the judgement call, and the compliance sign-off, stays with your team.
