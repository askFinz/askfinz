## Frequently asked

### What can a student do with askFinz?

A student can bring their course materials into askFinz, turning them into AI study tools, a lockdown exam simulator, and a knowledge bank. They can also research studies by synthesizing and referencing sources, and manage their ideas using version control and merging. Additionally, they can connect their existing tools and knowledge layers, and use askFinz to pull them into one searchable, permission-aware knowledge layer.

### How does askFinz help with research?

AskFinz allows students to research studies by turning them into notebooks that read, synthesize, and reference. It supports version control, merging findings, and provides multi-format output, ensuring that students can explore multiple angles on a question, combine findings, and generate accurate and verifiable citations.

### Are the results reliable enough to act on?

Every summary, quiz, and exam stays on-topic and is grounded in your own material, ensuring reliable progress analytics.
