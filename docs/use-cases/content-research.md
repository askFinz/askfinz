## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz help research and fact-check a piece before it's written?

Yes. Pull reporting, data and prior coverage into a research notebook; the workspace drafts a synthesis with a citation on each claim, so a fact-check is a click back to the source rather than a re-search.

### Does it track what's already been covered?

Yes. Deduped, multi-source news clusters show what's already out there on a topic, so a new piece can add an angle instead of repeating the wire.

### Can I keep a standing research file for a beat?

Yes. A notebook per beat or topic accumulates sourced detail over time, and live news keeps it current between full rewrites.
