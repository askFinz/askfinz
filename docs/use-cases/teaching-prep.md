## Frequently asked

### Can I generate quizzes and summaries from my own lecture material?

Yes. Learn reads the material you bring in — slides, readings, lecture notes — page by page, and can generate summaries, quizzes, flashcards and other study tools scoped to a specific unit or slide range.

### Can I research a supporting example or reference for a lecture?

Yes. A research notebook lets you pull in current sources and drafts a synthesis with a citation on each claim, so you can bring an up-to-date example into class with its source attached.

### Does this replace my own course design?

No. It turns your own material into study tools and helps you research supporting content — the syllabus, the pedagogy and what to teach stay your call.
