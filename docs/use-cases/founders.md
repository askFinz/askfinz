## Frequently asked

### What can a founder do with askFinz?

Dash provides an internal control panel for askFinz operators and account admins, offering role-based permissions, audit logs, real-time observability, and billing and plan administration. Finance offers a global markets terminal with live prices, deep data, AI forecasts, and research, including company financials, AI price forecasts, and portfolio and risk tools. Projects offers a project tool with kanban, scrum, and issue links, dependencies, burndown, and automations. Workflow provides a visual pipeline builder for drag, drop, and AI call retries, with triggers, steps, and retry logic.

### How does askFinz help with planning?

Projects, sprints, boards and issues provide a project tool that knows the rest of your work. With dependencies, burndown, automations and cross-project roll-ups, you can plan and manage your projects effectively.

### Are the results reliable enough to act on?

Every figure links back to the filing or call it came from, so you can verify each one in a click.
