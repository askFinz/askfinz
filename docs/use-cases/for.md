## Key points

### Eight patterns, twenty-three jobs

Every page in the set is one of these work patterns. If the job is not listed by name, it is almost certainly one of these shapes.

### AI for equity research

Build a defensible equity research note in an afternoon — pull 10-K passages, earnings calls and news into one workspace where every figure cites its source.

### AI for due diligence

Run a consistent diligence pass over every target — the same rubric, the same source breadth, the same length each time, every finding traceable.

### AI for market research

Turn a market question into a sourced brief — reports, filings and multi-source news in one searchable workspace, every claim traced to its origin.

### AI for competitive analysis

Track competitors in one workspace — filings, releases and news synthesised into a living battlecard, every claim linked to the source behind it.

### AI for financial analysts

One workspace for the analyst's day — morning briefing, citation-backed notes, watchlist news and a searchable record of everything you've read.

### AI for consultants

Research, synthesise and write the deliverable in one workspace — turn a client question into a sourced, defensible brief without the four-tool scramble.

### AI for literature reviews

Run a literature review in one workspace — synthesise papers into a sourced summary, search everything you have read, keep every claim cited.

### AI coding assistant for developers

Code with AI across the web IDE, your browser and the desktop app — one assistant, your files and history, available wherever you work.

### AI for clinical research

Search registered clinical trials by condition or phase, read them beside the published literature, and file a sourced summary traced to the record.
