## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz search clinical trial registries?

Yes. The index holds registered studies with their sponsor, phase and status attached, searchable by condition, intervention or population rather than by trial name alone.

### Does it read trials alongside the published literature?

Yes. Trial records sit beside the academic corpus in the same index, so a study's registered design and the papers written about it turn up together and can be pulled into one notebook.

### Is this a clinical decision-support tool?

No. askFinz surfaces the registered trial record and the literature around it with citations attached — it doesn't diagnose, recommend treatment or replace a clinician's judgement.
