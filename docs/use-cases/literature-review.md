## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz summarise academic papers with citations?

Yes. Bring the papers into a notebook and the workspace drafts a synthesis that keeps each claim linked to the paper it came from, so the review is traceable rather than paraphrased away from its sources.

### Can I search across all my papers at once?

Yes. Everything you collect becomes a semantic index, so you can find a finding, method or quote across the whole corpus without re-reading each PDF.

### Does it keep versions as I revise?

Filed reviews and sources are versioned, so each revision branches from a stable base instead of overwriting the previous draft.
