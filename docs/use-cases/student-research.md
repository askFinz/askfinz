## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz help me research and write an essay with proper citations?

Yes. A research notebook drafts a synthesis from the sources you bring in or gather, and keeps a citation on every claim, so you can check and format each reference rather than reconstructing your bibliography afterwards.

### Does it work with my actual course material?

Yes. Learn reads material synced from your course — lectures, slides and readings — and produces summaries and study tools grounded in what your class actually covers.

### Will using this count as plagiarism?

askFinz cites its sources rather than hiding them, but the essay you submit is still your own work to write and check against your institution's academic-integrity policy — treat it as a research and drafting aid, not a substitute for doing the assignment.
