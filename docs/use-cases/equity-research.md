## Frequently asked

### Does askFinz cite its sources for equity research?

Yes. Research notebooks attach a citation to each claim, so every figure in a note links back to the filing, transcript or article it came from — the point is a memo you can defend, not a black box.

### Can I use my own filings and transcripts?

Yes. Upload the documents you already collect and they become part of a searchable index the notebook reads from, alongside live multi-source news.

### Is this a replacement for my data terminal or order-management system?

No. askFinz produces the note, the briefing and the searchable record — it doesn't hold positions, route trades or replace your book of record.
