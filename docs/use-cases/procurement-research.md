## Frequently asked

### Can askFinz search public contract notices?

Yes. Contract notices are searchable by what you supply, in plain language, rather than by the procurement category or reference number alone.

### Does it show the full notice or just a summary?

The published notice itself is available, including the detail — deadlines, requirements, contact — that decides whether it's worth a bid.

### Can I track a specific buyer or category over time?

Yes. Keep a standing notebook on a buyer or a category and new notices accumulate there, so a weekly check is a refresh of an existing file rather than a fresh search.
