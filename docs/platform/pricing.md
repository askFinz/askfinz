## Key points

### Add more when you need it

Optional extras that sit on top of whatever plan you're on — billed separately, cancel any time.

### Cheaper than everyone. By a lot

$ 0.020 /GB storage and zero egress fees — backed by a 2-tier EU redundancy system that switches over automatically in under 60 seconds.

### How your data is protected

Your files are written to primary storage and simultaneously mirrored every 60 seconds to an independent live backup in a separate EU facility. If primary storage ever goes offline, the backup activates automatically — no manual steps, no restarts, no data loss.

### Our own index, so our own price

Most AI tools pay a third party for every web search and pass the bill on. We read the web ourselves, so searching it costs a fraction of the going rate.

### Marketplace & travel

## Frequently asked

### Is annual billing cheaper?

Yes — every paid plan is priced lower when you pay yearly instead of twelve monthly payments. You'll see the annual figure on each plan at checkout.

### Are there any egress or download fees?

No. Downloading your files from askFinz is always free — no egress charges, ever. We built on a provider partnership that makes outbound delivery cost us nothing, so we pass that directly to you.

### What does Enterprise add?

SAML SSO, audit logs, an isolated data perimeter, dedicated compute, askFinz OS device management and self-host options — shaped to your organisation. It's custom-priced; talk to us.
