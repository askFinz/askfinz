# Zoom integration: search meeting transcripts

Bring Zoom into askFinz Knowledge so meeting transcripts and recordings are searchable in askFinz. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/zoom](https://askfinz.com/integrations/zoom)

## Key points

### Find the moment a decision was made

Ask what was agreed about a topic and get the passage from the transcript, with the meeting and date, so you can jump to that point in the recording.

### Catch up on a call you missed

Ask what a meeting covered and get an answer grounded in what was actually said, cited to the transcript rather than summarised from nothing.

### Search calls next to documents and tickets

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…

### What crosses, and what stays in Zoom

Comes in Read transcripts and meeting titles for the recordings you choose to include.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to cloud recordings and transcripts for the accounts an administrator includes — no ability to join, start or change a meeting.
