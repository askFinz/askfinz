## Key points

### Answer a ticket with the ticket that already solved it

Describe the problem in your own words and get the earlier ticket that matches it — including the reply that resolved it and the steps that did not work.

### See what customers actually keep asking for

The pattern in a quarter of tickets is not in the tags; it is in the words customers used. Searching by meaning groups the complaints that describe the same problem in five different ways.

### Keep the help centre honest

Ask what the documentation says and what tickets say about the same feature. Where those two disagree, you have found the article that needs rewriting.

### What crosses, and what stays in Zendesk

Comes in Read ticket subjects, conversations, tags and help-centre articles from the brands you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access through an agent account scoped to the brands and groups you choose — enough to read tickets, never enough to reply to one.
