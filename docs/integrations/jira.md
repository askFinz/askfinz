# Jira integration: search tickets and comments

Bring Jira into askFinz Knowledge so you can search tickets, track blockers, and discuss sprint health in plain language. Coming soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/jira](https://askfinz.com/integrations/jira)

## Key points

### Find a ticket by what was discussed, not by its fields

Ask "the ticket where we decided not to support offline mode" and get it, even if nobody wrote "offline mode" in the summary. Comments are searched by meaning, which is the part JQL cannot do.

### Answer "has this come up before?" in one question

Recurring bugs and repeated requests are expensive precisely because the previous round is hard to find. Connected, the earlier ticket surfaces with its resolution attached.

### Carry sprint context into a document

Writing a status update or a post-mortem means pulling context back out of the board. Ask in Research and the answer cites the issue keys, so the reader can open each one.

### What crosses, and what stays in Jira

Comes in Read issue summaries, descriptions, comments and status from the projects you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the projects you choose, honouring the same project and issue-level permissions your Jira site already enforces.
