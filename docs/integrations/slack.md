# Slack integration: find decisions in threads

Bring Slack into askFinz Knowledge so decisions buried in threads are searchable and citable in Research and Chat. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/slack](https://askfinz.com/integrations/slack)

## Key points

### Recover a decision from the thread it happened in

Ask what the team agreed about a topic and get the thread, with the channel and date, so you can read the rest of the conversation around it.

### Stop re-asking a question that was answered in March

The same operational question gets asked every few months because nobody can find the answer. Matched by meaning, the old thread comes back even when it used different words.

### Keep new joiners out of your DMs

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…

### What crosses, and what stays in Slack

Comes in Read messages and thread replies in the channels you add the connection to.

### What you will be granting

Read access to the specific channels someone invites askFinz into — the same model as any other Slack app, and it can be removed from a channel in one command.
