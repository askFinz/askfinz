Bring Proton Drive into askFinz Knowledge so your privacy-first files are searchable, without compromising control. Coming to askFinz soon.

## Key points

### Search privacy-first files without giving up the reason you chose them

The point of connecting is to make your own files findable to you — not to move them somewhere less protected, and not to widen who can open them.

### Choose file by file, not folder by default

The default is that nothing is included. You add what you want searchable, and you can remove it again, which also removes its searchable copy.

### Keep the rest of the drive untouched

### What crosses, and what stays in Proton Drive

Comes in Read only the files you explicitly select and unlock at the moment you add them.

### What you will be granting

Access to the specific files you choose, at the moment you choose them, and nothing standing — you are handing over documents, not the keys to the drive.
