# Google Drive integration: search by content

Bring Google Drive into askFinz Knowledge so every doc, sheet, and slide is searchable in Chat and Research. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/google-drive](https://askfinz.com/integrations/google-drive)

## Key points

### Find a document by what is inside it

Describe the content — the argument, the number, the client — rather than the filename, and get the document, named and linked so you can open it in Drive.

### Read Docs, Sheets and Slides as content

A point made on slide 14 or a note in a sheet's comment column is content, not an attachment. Those are searched as text, so a deck is as findable as a doc.

### Keep everyone's access exactly as it is

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…

### What crosses, and what stays in Drive

Comes in Read the text of documents in the folders and shared drives you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the folders and shared drives you pick, with no permission to create, change or share anything in Drive.
