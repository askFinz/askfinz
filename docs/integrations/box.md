# Box integration: search contracts and files

Bring Box into askFinz Knowledge so every folder, document, and contract is searchable in Chat and Research. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/box](https://askfinz.com/integrations/box)

## Key points

### Find a clause across every contract at once

Ask about a term and get the agreements that contain it, each named and linked, instead of opening thirty PDFs to check.

### Ask what a document actually says

Answers quote the file and cite it, so a colleague can open the original and verify the wording rather than trusting a summary.

### Keep folder permissions doing their job

### What crosses, and what stays in Box

Comes in Read the text of documents in the folders you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the folders you choose, honouring Box's existing collaborator permissions — no ability to change, share or delete anything.
