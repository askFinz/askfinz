# Microsoft Teams integration: search channels

Bring Microsoft Teams into askFinz Knowledge so channels, chats, and meeting notes are searchable in Research and Chat. Coming to askFinz soon.

## Key points

### Put the conversation back next to the document

Ask about a project and get both: the channel thread where the approach was argued out, and the file it produced, each linked.

### Find a channel post by what it was about

Channel search is keyword matching across a lot of noise. Matching by meaning finds the post that answers your question even when it shares none of your words.

### Search meeting notes with everything else

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…

### What crosses, and what stays in Teams

Comes in Read channel posts, replies and channel-hosted notes for the teams you select.

### What you will be granting

Read access to the specific teams and channels an administrator approves, honouring the membership rules already set in Microsoft 365.
