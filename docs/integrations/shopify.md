# Shopify integration: search orders and products

Bring Shopify into askFinz Knowledge so every order, product, and customer record is searchable in plain language. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/shopify](https://askfinz.com/integrations/shopify)

## Key points

### Ask why returns cluster on one product

Return reasons, order notes and customer messages are text, and text is where the cause is. Searching them by meaning groups the complaints that describe the same fault in different words.

### Look a customer up in one question

Order history, notes and previous contacts answered together, so support does not stitch three tabs into a reply.

### Search the catalogue the way a customer describes it

Product descriptions and variants are matched by meaning, so "the waterproof one in navy" finds the SKU without anyone knowing the SKU.

### What crosses, and what stays in Shopify

Comes in Read products, collections, order details, notes and customer records from the store you connect.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the object types you tick — you can connect the catalogue without connecting customers, or both — using a private app the store owner can revoke.
