# BigCommerce integration: search your catalogue

Bring BigCommerce into askFinz Knowledge so every product, order, and customer record is searchable in plain language. Coming soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/bigcommerce](https://askfinz.com/integrations/bigcommerce)

## Key points

### Search the catalogue by description, not by SKU

Ask for "the outdoor range with the two-year warranty" and get the products, including the ones whose titles say nothing about either.

### Ask why a product line underperforms

Order notes, return reasons and customer messages carry the cause. Matched by meaning, five differently worded complaints about the same fault come back as one group.

### Answer a customer question from their own history

Orders, notes and past contacts for one customer, answered together instead of assembled by hand across tabs.

### What crosses, and what stays in BigCommerce

Comes in Read products, categories, custom fields, order details and customer records you enable.
