# Trello integration: search every board

Bring Trello into askFinz Knowledge so every board, card, and checklist is searchable with AI. Coming to askFinz soon.

## Key points

### Search across every board at once

Ask a question and get the card that answers it, whichever board it is on, named so you can open it.

### Read the card backs, not just the titles

Descriptions, comments and checklist items carry the real content. Those are matched by meaning, so "the client who wanted the packaging changed" finds the card even if it is titled "Rev 3".

### Bring an archived board back into use

Archived boards are where the last campaign's plan lives. Connected, they answer questions instead of sitting in a list nobody opens.

### What crosses, and what stays in Trello

Comes in Read card titles, descriptions, comments, checklists and labels from the boards you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the boards you tick, with the same visibility your own Trello account already has.
