# Notion integration: search pages and databases

Bring Notion into askFinz Knowledge so your wikis, databases, and notes are searchable and citable in Chat and Research. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/notion](https://askfinz.com/integrations/notion)

## Key points

### Search inside nested pages and toggles

Content buried in sub-pages, callouts and collapsed toggles is read as content, not as structure, so the paragraph that answers the question surfaces even when its parent page is titled something unhelpful.

### Ask a database a question in words

Database rows carry properties and long text. Both are usable: "which of our vendors are on annual terms" narrows by property, while "the one who kept missing deadlines" matches what people actually wrote in the notes.

### Cite the page, not a paraphrase

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…
