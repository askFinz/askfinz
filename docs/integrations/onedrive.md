# OneDrive integration: search your own files

Bring OneDrive into askFinz Knowledge so every file is searchable and usable in Chat and Research. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/onedrive](https://askfinz.com/integrations/onedrive)

## Key points

### Find your own work by describing it

Ask for the analysis you wrote about a supplier, and get the file, even when it is called "notes v2 final".

### Read Word, Excel and PowerPoint as content

The number in a spreadsheet cell and the argument on slide 14 are searched as text, so a deck is as findable as a document.

### Use your files without publishing them

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…

### What crosses, and what stays in OneDrive

Comes in Read the text of documents in the folders you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the folders you pick in your own OneDrive, with no permission to change or share anything in it.
