# SharePoint integration: search the intranet

Bring SharePoint into askFinz Knowledge so your intranet, wikis, and shared libraries are searchable in Chat and Research. Coming to askFinz soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/sharepoint](https://askfinz.com/integrations/sharepoint)

## Key points

### Ask the intranet a question and get one answer

Instead of a results page across four team sites, get the answer with the page or document it came from named, so you can check it.

### Search libraries and pages together

Document libraries and site pages hold different halves of the same subject. Reading both means the policy and the form that implements it come back to the same question.

### Keep site permissions exactly as they are

ONE QUESTION “What did we decide about pay bands?” asked by two colleagues In the finance group could already open all three Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck Comp bands (finance only) Not in the finance group could never open the third Q3 planning notes Pricing review deck — not in these…

### What crosses, and what stays in SharePoint

Comes in Read site pages and document text from the sites and libraries you select.

### What you will be granting

Read-only access to the sites and libraries an administrator approves, honouring the permissions Microsoft 365 already enforces on each one.
