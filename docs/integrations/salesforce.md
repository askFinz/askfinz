# Salesforce integration: search your CRM

Bring Salesforce into askFinz Knowledge so every account, opportunity, and contact is searchable in plain language. Coming soon.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations/salesforce](https://askfinz.com/integrations/salesforce)

## Key points

### Ask an account for its own history

"What happened the last time we renewed this account?" gets answered from the opportunity, the activities and the notes attached to it — with each record named, so you can open it in Salesforce and check.

### Find the reasoning in notes and closed-lost reasons

Free-text is where the honesty lives. Those fields are matched by meaning, so a search for "lost on procurement delays" can surface a note that never used those words.

### Prepare for a call without a report request

ONE RECORD’S FIELDS WHAT IT BECOMES WHAT THAT LETS YOU ASK Name, title, reference the short columns Text you can match by meaning, not spelling “the renewal we agreed in March” Notes, descriptions, comments the long columns Text you can quote answers cite the record “why was this one discounted?”…

### What crosses, and what stays in Salesforce

### What you will be granting
