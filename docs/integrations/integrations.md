# Integrations: search Slack, Drive, Notion and more

Connect the tools your work already lives in — Drive, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Salesforce and more — so you can search, cite and use them across askFinz.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/integrations](https://askfinz.com/integrations)

## Key points

### File storage

Search the folders you picked the way you would ask a colleague, instead of guessing at filenames.

### Docs & wikis

Ask a question of your pages and wikis, and get the answer cited back to the page it came from.

### Chat & meetings

Find the decision that happened in a thread months ago, without remembering the exact words.

### Code

Read repositories, pull requests and the docs beside them as one body of work.

### Projects & issues

Tickets, projects and the reasoning behind them, answerable in one search.

### CRM

Ask a question of account history instead of scrolling through it.

### Support

Turn the tickets you have already answered into an answer key.

### Design

Files, frames and the comments that explain why a design changed.

### Analytics

Dashboards and their definitions, searchable next to everything else.

### Commerce

Catalogue, orders and customer history in the same place you research.
