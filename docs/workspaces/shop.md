# AI storefront built into your workspace

AI marketplace: browse across storefronts, describe what you need, get a ready cart. Checkout, orders and shipping across 37 carriers.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/apps/shop](https://askfinz.com/apps/shop)

## Key points

### Describe what you want. The AI builds the cart

Shop is a multi-vendor marketplace with an AI shopping assistant at its centre. Browse trending products and maker storefronts the usual way — or describe what you're trying to do in one sentence and let the assistant assemble a ready cart across whichever vendors stock the right items.

### Browse, describe, cart — paid in one place

A scripted walk-through — products across multiple vendors, a cart filling item by item, checkout flipping to paid, and the order pipeline lighting up. Try the live marketplace at shop.askfinz.ai.

### Where you can ship

Every coloured country is one your buyers can receive in — nearly the whole world. The deeper-shaded markets are where a seller also has a domestic carrier to ship from.

### Carriers you can ship with

A seller anywhere can ship to a buyer anywhere. Pick the carrier that fits your lane — domestic or cross-border — get live rates, buy the label through the platform, and the buyer gets a tracking number automatically.

### Tracking we connect with

Even where a seller didn't print the label with us, we still follow these postal and regional networks end to end — so order status and delivery updates stay live wherever the parcel travels.

### What it costs to sell

Priced to match or beat the lowest commission in every category — and unlike a bare storefront, askFinz brings you the buyers. New sellers start at just 1 % for the first 3 months.

### How we protect buyers & sellers

Every order runs through one protected pipeline. Buyers are covered until the parcel is in their hands; sellers are paid as each order clears its short return window, and are shielded from fraud and chargebacks.

### Why sell & shop on askFinz

askFinz isn't a storefront bolted onto a payments box — it's a marketplace that lives inside a whole workspace. That's what lets it shop for you, charge less, protect both sides, and ship the world without a single add-on to install.

### Analytics built in

Every store gets a full analytics suite — the kind you'd bolt on with three paid apps elsewhere — explained in plain English, from real-time visitors right through to net profit per product.

### What ships today

Shop is in beta. These features are live but still being refined based on early user feedback.
