## Frequently asked

### What exactly is askFinz?

askFinz is an AI platform with specialized workspaces for different types of work — research, code, finance, health, data analysis, and more. Instead of one chatbot trying to do everything, you get purpose-built agents that excel at specific tasks. They all share memory and work together when needed.

### How many agents are there?

The platform includes 50+ specialized agents organized into groups: foundational orchestration, research, code, chat, news, search, workflow automation, and more. Each agent is designed for a specific job — from planning and routing to deep research, code review, and web browsing.

### Which AI models can I use?

askFinz routes across ten model providers — OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Moonshot, NVIDIA NIM, Z.ai and Google Cloud — spanning frontier, open-weight and specialist models. The platform automatically routes to the best model for each task, or you can choose manually. No vendor lock-in.

### What makes the agents different from regular chatbots?

Our agents are specialized and collaborative. The Researcher cites every claim. The Coder understands your entire repository. The Navigator operates a real browser. They pause for your approval on important decisions, hand work between each other, and maintain context across all your workspaces.

### How does privacy and data security work?

Sensitive workspaces like Med run on isolated databases with separate credentials, and all data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We don't train our models on your data without explicit opt-in — you stay in control of what you share. Enterprise customers get additional security controls and audit trails.

### Is the beta free?

Yes. The private beta is free while we gather feedback and refine the platform. Pricing for general availability will be announced before launch, and beta participants get priority access plus launch credits as a thank you.

### What's included in each workspace?

Each workspace is purpose-built: Research has citation tools and branched notebooks. Code has a full IDE with git integration and a pair-programming agent. News aggregates and deduplicates across many sources with a daily audio briefing. Stream is a creator platform for video, music and live broadcasts. All workspaces share the same memory layer and can call each other's agents.

### How does the agent routing work?

When you submit a prompt, the Thinker agent classifies your intent. The Planner breaks complex tasks into steps. The Router assigns each step to the best-fit specialist agent. The Executor runs the plan while streaming progress. The Reflector reviews quality before returning results to you.

### Can my team or organization use it?

Yes. askFinz is designed for both individuals and teams. Features include shared workspaces, team memory, role-based access control, audit trails, and usage analytics. Contact us for enterprise pricing and access options.

### What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export everything — conversations, documents, research notes, code projects, and all metadata. We don't lock you in. Your data is yours, and we provide standard export formats that work with other tools.

### How do I get access?

Request access through the beta signup form. We're onboarding in cohorts to ensure quality and gather feedback. Priority goes to users who can provide detailed feedback on specific workspaces they plan to use heavily.
